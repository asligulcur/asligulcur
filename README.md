## Selected work

- [Multi-agent procurement simulation](https://github.com/asligulcur/The_AI_Bidding_War_April_2026) — AI agents negotiate a software purchase under four safeguards; in a recorded run, the spending limit in code blocked an award 47% over budget, even after a human approved it.
- [Competing AI strategy agents](https://github.com/asligulcur/StockTrader_Signals_Strategies_2026) — three LLM agents analyze the same market data and disagree; agreement is checked in code, and an evaluator agent explains why.
- [Citation-grounded research portal](https://github.com/asligulcur/RAG-Research-Portal-Feb-2026) — RAG over 30 research papers; in the final GPT-4 evaluation runs, all 171 citations pointed to a real paper and a retrieved passage.
- [Humanitarian funding-gap monitor](https://github.com/asligulcur/humanitarian-funding-gap-monitor) — one of three winners (solo), Databricks × UN OCHA challenge at CMU; ranks humanitarian plans by unmet need against crisis severity.
- [Workforce adaptation simulation](https://github.com/asligulcur/AI_Workforce_Odyssey_2026) — an agent-based model showing that equal access to training does not produce equal recovery; runs in the browser.
- [Real-time monitoring pipeline](https://github.com/asligulcur/Real_TimeCryptoAIService_Nov_2025) — a streaming ML project exploring drift monitoring, observability, rollback, and model-lifecycle patterns.
